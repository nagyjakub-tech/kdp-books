## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** How I hit 160g protein/day on a genuinely tight grocery
budget (no protein powder math-gaming)

**Text:**
When I started lifting seriously, every "eat more protein" guide assumed I
had money for salmon and chicken breast three times a day. I don't. What
actually worked was building meals around the 4-5 proteins that are
reliably cheap per gram: eggs, canned tuna, dried beans/lentils, chicken
thighs (not breast), and cottage cheese when it's on sale.

My current staple: rice + a can of beans + an egg on top, under $1.50 a
plate and around 25g protein. Batch-cooked chicken thighs in the oven
once a week cover the rest. The trick isn't some hack, it's just refusing
to buy the "fitness influencer" proteins that are priced for people who
don't need to count dollars.

I ended up writing all these cheap-protein combos down into a little book
called *The Broke Bodybuilder's Cookbook* because I kept re-explaining the
same price-per-gram logic to gym friends — happy to break down the actual
cost-per-gram-of-protein numbers here if that's useful to anyone.
