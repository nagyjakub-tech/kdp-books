## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The "after work dinner" system that actually works (prep is the whole trick)

**Text:**
For months my after-work dinners were either delivery or standing over
the stove too tired to care. What finally fixed it wasn't a specific
recipe — it was prepping proteins and veggies into pre-portioned bags
on Sunday, so the air fryer was doing 100% of the active cooking on a
weeknight and I was doing basically nothing. Cut, season, bag, fridge.
Then it's just dump-basket-set timer-eat. Also learned the hard way that
preheating the basket for 2-3 minutes before adding food makes a bigger
difference on crispiness than almost any seasoning trick.

Ended up writing down the exact prep-ahead combos and timings that work
for a tired-after-work brain as *Air Fryer After Work* — mostly so future-me
wouldn't have to re-figure it out every Tuesday. Anyone else have a
Sunday-prep system that saved their weeknights?
