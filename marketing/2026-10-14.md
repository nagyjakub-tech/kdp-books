## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The parchment-sling trick that finally stopped my air fryer desserts from sticking and falling apart

**Telo príspevku:**

> I avoided doing desserts in the air fryer for ages because anything soft (cookie dough, banana fritters, that kind of thing) either stuck to the basket or collapsed when I tried to flip it halfway through. Two small changes fixed basically all of it.
>
> First: cut a parchment paper sling — a strip wide enough to line the bottom and come up both sides like handles — instead of a flat circle. You can lift the whole dessert out by the paper instead of fighting it out with a spatula, and nothing sticks to the mesh.
>
> Second: drop the temperature about 25°F below what the recipe says for frying and add a couple of minutes instead. Desserts have sugar that burns fast on the outside before the inside sets, so slightly lower and slower gets you an actual cooked center instead of a scorched shell over raw dough. My go-to now is cinnamon-sugar apple slices at 325°F for 10 minutes, flipped once with the parchment sling — crispy edges, soft middle, zero mess on the basket. I ended up collecting a bunch of these dessert-specific adjustments (since most air fryer recipes online are written for savory food, not sweets) into a short book called "Air Fryer Desserts." Happy to share more temp/time pairings if anyone's fighting a specific recipe.
