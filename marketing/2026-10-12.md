## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The "just use what's already frozen" method that fixed my weeknight dinner problem

**Telo príspevku:**

> I used to think air frying frozen food meant fries and chicken nuggets and that was it. Turns out almost the entire freezer aisle works if you stop treating the bag instructions as gospel and learn a couple of general rules instead.
>
> The two things that changed everything: (1) group by shape, not by brand — frozen veggies, breaded proteins, and pre-formed patties each have their own rough time/temp range, so once you know the range you can air fry basically any new frozen item without googling it first; (2) don't dump a full bag in cold — split it across two batches if it's more than a single layer, because an overloaded basket just steams the bottom layer instead of crisping it. My current go-to "I have no plan" dinner is a bag of frozen stir-fry veggies plus frozen breaded chicken, both in the basket at 400°F, veggies in at minute 6 so they finish together with the chicken.
>
> I ended up writing all my frozen-aisle timing notes down into a short cookbook called "Air Fryer Frozen Aisle Hacks" because I kept wanting to text myself the chart. Happy to share specific times for whatever's in your freezer if anyone's stuck.
