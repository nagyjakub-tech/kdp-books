## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** My Sunday lunch-prep system that finally keeps me full until dinner (and off DoorDash)

**Telo príspevku:**

> For years my work lunch was whatever was closest to the microwave, and by 3pm I was starving and ordering a $14 delivery snack to make it to dinner. What fixed it wasn't a diet, it was just prepping lunches that actually have enough protein and don't need reheating — because half the time there's a line for the office microwave anyway.
>
> My go-to rotation now: mason jar chicken + quinoa + chickpea salads (dressing on the side so nothing gets soggy by Wednesday), cold sesame soba noodles with edamame and shredded chicken, egg muffin cups with turkey bacon and spinach (grab two, done), and big batches of marinated tofu or steak strips over rice that taste fine cold or at room temp. I prep 4-5 containers every Sunday and just rotate through the week.
>
> The biggest lesson was portioning protein first (aim for 30g+ per box) and building the rest of the lunch around that, not the other way around. I ended up writing down all my favorite cold/room-temp high protein combos in a little book called "High Protein Lunchbox" after a coworker asked for my "lunch system" for the third time — happy to share specific recipes in the comments if anyone wants them.
