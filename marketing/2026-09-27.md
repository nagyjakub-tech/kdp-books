**Text:**
> One thing that hit my wallet hard when I started GLP-1 meds: I kept
> buying groceries for meals I could only eat a third of, then throwing
> the rest away. Nobody warns you that "eat whatever, just less" gets
> expensive fast once your appetite drops that much.
>
> What actually fixed it for me was switching to a short list of cheap,
> protein-dense staples I already knew portion well in small amounts —
> eggs, canned tuna, dried beans, frozen vegetables — and building meals
> around single servings instead of family-size batches that go bad in
> the fridge. Protein first, small plate, nothing perishable I can't
> finish in one sitting.
>
> I put the specific recipes and swaps that worked for me into a book
> called *GLP-1 on a Budget*, if that's useful to anyone else watching
> both their appetite and their grocery bill — but the core trick (cheap
> shelf-stable protein, single-serving portions) is worth trying
> regardless of what you end up cooking.
