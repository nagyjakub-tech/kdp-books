## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1 alebo r/loseit)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** What nobody tells you about GLP-1 + being over 50 at the same time (appetite loss stacked on muscle loss)

**Telo príspevku:**

> Started this medication in my 50s and the thing that caught me off guard wasn't the nausea everyone warns you about — it was realizing my "eat less" problem was colliding with a "already losing muscle from age" problem I'd been ignoring for years. Eating almost nothing felt fine day to day, but I could tell strength and energy were quietly draining too.
>
> What helped was shrinking portions instead of skipping meals, and making sure almost everything on the plate had protein in it even in tiny amounts — a boiled egg instead of toast, cottage cheese instead of a granola bar, broth-based soup with shredded chicken instead of crackers. None of it is exotic, it's just protein-first eating scaled down to what a smaller appetite can actually finish.
>
> I wrote up the combinations that worked for me at this age and stage into a short cookbook, "GLP-1 for Women Over 50" — sharing here because most GLP-1 content online assumes a 30-year-old's appetite and metabolism, and this sub seems to get that the over-50 version of this is a different problem. The portion-shrinking + protein-first approach above works whether or not you ever look at the book.
