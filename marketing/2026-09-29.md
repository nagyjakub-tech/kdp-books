**Titulok:** How I stopped feeling like GLP-1 meant giving up comfort food

**Text:**
> One thing that got to me early on GLP-1 wasn't the nausea, it was the
> feeling that every "safe" meal was boring — chicken and rice, chicken
> and rice, forever. Comfort food felt off-limits because a full-size
> portion of mac and cheese or shepherd's pie just isn't realistic
> anymore, and most recipes online aren't written for that.
>
> What actually helped was rebuilding a few classic comfort dishes at
> maybe a third of the normal size, with the protein bumped up and the
> heavy starch/fat trimmed back — same flavor memory, portion that
> actually fits. A mini shepherd's pie with extra lean ground turkey, a
> small ramekin of "mac and cheese" with a cottage-cheese-based sauce,
> that kind of thing.
>
> I put a bunch of these together in a book called *GLP-1 Comfort Food
> Rebuilt* in case it's useful to anyone here — but even without it, the
> core idea is free: don't cut comfort food, just rebuild it smaller and
> higher-protein so it fits what you can actually eat now.
