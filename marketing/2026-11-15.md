## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** Finally stopped halving family-size air fryer recipes after doing the math wrong one too many times

**Text:**
Cooking for two in an air fryer basket sized for a family sounds like it
should "just work" if you halve the recipe, but it kept going wrong for
me: half the ingredients at the same time/temp either overcooked (less
food = faster browning, more empty basket space for hot air to hit it
directly) or stayed weirdly undercooked in the middle because I was
crowding two half-portions instead of actually single-layering them.

What actually fixed it: (1) drop the time by roughly 15-20%, not 50%,
when you halve the food — mass goes down but exposed surface area per
item barely changes. (2) keep the basket at the same single-layer rule
regardless of batch size — two portions in a bigger empty basket browns
faster than four portions packed in, so check 3-4 minutes earlier than
the original recipe says. (3) shake/flip at the halfway point more
aggressively for small batches since there's less food mass holding heat
evenly.

Wrote down the times/temps that actually worked for two-person portions
(chicken thighs, salmon, roasted veg, frozen stuff) as *Air Fryer for
Two* — mostly so I'd stop re-doing this math every week. Curious if
anyone else cooking for just 1-2 people has found different
adjustments that work better than mine.
