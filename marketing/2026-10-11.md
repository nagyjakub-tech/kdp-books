## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** I kept ruining air fryer recipes as a total beginner until I figured out these 3 things — sharing in case it saves someone else

**Telo príspevku:**

> When I got my air fryer I assumed it was just "oven but faster" and kept getting either raw-in-the-middle chicken or rock-hard fries. Turns out most of my mistakes were basic stuff nobody tells you on the box.
>
> Three things that fixed almost everything: (1) preheat for real — 3-4 minutes empty, not just "turn it on and throw food in," because a cold basket adds 5+ minutes of uneven cooking; (2) don't trust the recipe's time as gospel, trust the shake/flip — pull the basket out at the halfway point every single time, even for stuff you think doesn't need it; (3) pat everything dry first, even "dry" frozen food, because surface moisture is what turns crispy into steamed-and-sad. My easiest fallback meal now is frozen chicken tenders at 400°F for 12 minutes, flipped at 6 — basically can't mess it up.
>
> I got tired of re-learning this trial and error for every new recipe so I put together a dead-simple beginner cookbook called "Air Fryer Zero Clue" — built around exactly this kind of can't-mess-it-up approach. Happy to answer questions if anyone's just starting out too.
