## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The 3 ingredients I always buy on sale that make broke-budget air frying actually work

**Telo príspevku:**

> I kept seeing "just air fry chicken breast" as the default budget advice, but chicken breast isn't actually the cheapest protein most weeks — it just gets recommended because it's easy. What's made the biggest difference for my grocery bill is building meals around whatever's the loss-leader that week (usually chicken thighs, eggs, or canned beans) and leaning on the air fryer to make cheap cuts taste like they cost more.
>
> Frozen mixed veg tossed straight from the freezer at 400°F for 12-14 minutes, shaken once, comes out way better than steaming and costs pennies per serving. Canned chickpeas, drained and patted dry, then air fried at 390°F for 15 minutes with whatever spice is already in your cabinet, make a crunchy snack/side that replaces chips for basically nothing. And bone-in chicken thighs on sale (almost always cheaper per pound than breast) crisp up at 380°F for 22-25 minutes with zero added oil needed.
>
> The real trick isn't a specific recipe, it's shopping the sale flyer first and then deciding what to cook, instead of picking a recipe and buying whatever it calls for. I put together a whole batch of budget-first air fryer recipes built that way into a book called "Air Fryer on a Broke Budget" if anyone wants the full list, but honestly just flipping that shopping order around helps immediately either way.
