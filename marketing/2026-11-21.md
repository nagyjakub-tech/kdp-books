## Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Titulok:** Stopped plating "normal" dinner portions and it fixed how
much I actually ate

**Text:**
For months I kept making full-size dinners out of habit, then either
forcing half of it down or giving up and barely eating anything. What
actually worked was switching to deliberately small plates — like a
side-plate instead of a dinner plate — and building each one around one
protein source plus one easy veggie, nothing else competing for stomach
space. A 3oz piece of salmon with a few roasted green beans felt
finishable in a way a "real" dinner plate never did, and I stopped
leaving food untouched.

I ended up turning the combos that worked for me into a little
collection called *GLP-1 Small Plates* — happy to share a few of the
exact pairings here if anyone else is struggling with portions feeling
too big right now.
