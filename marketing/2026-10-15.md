## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** Why halving a 4-serving air fryer recipe's time doesn't work (and what to do instead)

**Telo príspevku:**

> Cooking for two, I kept taking normal air fryer recipes (almost always written for 4 servings) and just eyeballing a smaller portion with the same time and temp. Results were all over the place — sometimes undercooked, sometimes dried out. Took me a while to figure out why.
>
> The problem isn't the amount of food, it's the basket fill. A recipe's time assumes a certain layer thickness and airflow around the food. Drop the quantity but keep it piled the same way (because it's a smaller basket or you're not spreading it out) and the airflow doesn't actually improve much — so you get inconsistent results for a different reason than you'd expect.
>
> What actually works: keep the temperature exactly as written, spread the smaller portion into a single loose layer (don't just dump it in the same shape), and only then cut the time — usually by about 15-20%, not 50%. For something like chicken thighs for two, that's 380°F for about 18 minutes instead of 22, shaking once at the halfway point, instead of guessing. I ended up writing down all these smaller-batch timing adjustments for the meals I actually cook on repeat into a little book called "Air Fryer for Two" — happy to share specific timing if anyone's trying to scale down a particular recipe.
