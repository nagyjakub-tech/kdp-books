## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The thing nobody warned me about on GLP-1 — losing strength, not just weight (and what actually helped)

**Telo príspevku:**

> About two months into treatment I noticed the scale moving the "right" direction but my strength at the gym was sliding fast — lighter weights felt heavier, and I was clearly losing muscle along with fat. Makes sense in hindsight: when you're eating 1,100 calories and barely finishing half a plate, protein is usually the first thing to fall short, not the last.
>
> What helped was flipping my meal planning order: protein target first, everything else built around whatever's left of my appetite. I now aim for 25-30g of protein in every single small meal (a couple eggs plus cottage cheese, a scoop of whey in a tiny smoothie, shredded chicken on a few crackers) instead of trying to fit protein into a "normal" meal I can't finish anyway. Tracking it for a week was eye-opening — I was closer to 40g a day before, not the 90g+ I actually need.
>
> I ended up writing down the low-volume, high-protein meals that worked for me as a short cookbook called "GLP-1 Muscle-Saver Cookbook" — mentioning it here because this sub deals with exactly this problem, not to push it on anyone. The protein-first planning trick above costs nothing and works with or without the book.
