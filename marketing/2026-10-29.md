## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1 alebo r/loseit)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The thing that got "comfort food" back on my plate on GLP-1

**Telo príspevku:**

> One of the hardest adjustments for me wasn't the lack of appetite itself — it was feeling like all the food I actually grew up loving (mac and cheese, shepherd's pie, chili) was suddenly "too heavy" and off the table. That's a rough trade when food is already harder to enjoy.
>
> What's worked for me: keep the dish recognizable, but cut the fat load that makes it sit heavy, swap in a protein boost (Greek yogurt instead of sour cream, extra lean ground turkey, cottage cheese blended into sauces), and shrink the portion so it's a real serving, not a "diet portion" that feels like punishment. Same comfort, way easier on the stomach, and it actually hits your protein target instead of just being empty carbs.
>
> I ended up writing down the versions that worked best for me as a short cookbook, "GLP-1 Comfort Food Rebuilt" — mentioning it here because most comfort food recipes online are written for people who can eat a full appetite's worth, not the smaller, gentler portions a lot of us need now. The rebuild approach above (keep it recognizable, cut the heaviness, boost the protein) works with or without the book.
