## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1 alebo r/loseit)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The "3 ingredients, no real cooking" rule that finally got my protein numbers up on GLP-1

**Telo príspevku:**

> For months my problem wasn't knowing what to eat on this medication, it was having zero energy or appetite to actually cook anything. By 6pm "cooking" felt like a part-time job I didn't sign up for, so I'd default to crackers or nothing — which obviously tanked my protein intake even further.
>
> What actually worked was giving myself a hard rule: any meal has to come together from 3 ingredients or fewer with no real cooking (microwave or no-heat only). Things like cottage cheese + a spoon of peanut butter + a few berries, or shredded rotisserie chicken straight from the store tub mixed with a little mayo and hot sauce. Sounds too simple to matter, but it's the difference between actually eating 70g of protein a day and eating 30g because everything else felt like too much effort.
>
> I collected the combinations that got me through the lowest-energy weeks into a short cookbook, "GLP-1 High Protein Low Effort" — mentioning it here because this sub gets the "I know what I should eat, I just can't make myself cook it" problem better than most, not trying to push it on anyone. The 3-ingredients-no-cooking rule above costs nothing and works with or without the book.
