## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** Cooking for one in the air fryer took me months to get right — here's what actually works (no more soggy single portions)

**Telo príspevku:**

> When I first got my air fryer I kept scaling down recipes built for 4 people and ending up with either burnt edges or a soggy middle, because most recipes assume a full basket. Turns out single portions need completely different timing and basket placement, not just "divide by 4."
>
> What finally worked: never fill more than half the basket (airflow needs room even for one portion), shake/flip at the halfway mark instead of waiting until the end, and pat proteins fully dry before seasoning — wet surfaces are the #1 reason single portions go soggy since there's less mass to drive off moisture. My go-to weeknight combo is a single chicken thigh at 380°F for 12 minutes with a quick flip at 6, paired with whatever frozen veg is in the freezer tossed in for the last 5 minutes — one basket, one plate, no extra pans.
>
> I ended up writing down all the single-portion timing charts I built through trial and error (by protein type, not just "chicken" or "fish") because I kept losing the sticky note — put them together in a little book called "Air Fryer for One." Happy to share the chicken thigh timing chart in the comments if anyone else is tired of guessing.
