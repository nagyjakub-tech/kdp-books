## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** The "5-minute protein breakfast" formula that actually keeps me
full until lunch (no protein shakes)

**Text:**
I used to skip breakfast or grab a granola bar because I was convinced a
real protein breakfast meant 20 minutes of cooking and dishes. Turns out
the fix was just picking ingredients that are already basically done —
not cooking faster.

My go-to now: Greek yogurt + a scoop of peanut butter + whatever fruit is
about to go bad (2 min, ~25g protein), or a "jar" breakfast I prep the
night before — cottage cheese, chia seeds, a bit of honey — ready to eat
cold straight out of the fridge. On busier mornings it's just 2 hard-boiled
eggs (made in batches on Sunday) plus a string cheese, which is under a
minute of actual effort. None of these need a stove in the morning, and
they're all cheaper per gram of protein than a shake.

I collected a bunch of these zero-cook and near-zero-cook combos into a
little cookbook called *Protein Breakfast in 5 Minutes* since I kept
texting the same three ideas to people — happy to list more of them here
if anyone wants specific combos for their macros.
