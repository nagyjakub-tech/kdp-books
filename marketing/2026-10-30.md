## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The mental shift that actually got me hitting my protein target without "meal prepping like a bodybuilder"

**Telo príspevku:**

> For months I thought hitting a real protein number meant Sunday meal-prep marathons and a fridge full of identical containers. What actually worked was way lazier: pick 4-5 "protein anchors" you always have around (eggs, Greek yogurt, canned beans, rotisserie or pre-cooked chicken, cottage cheese) and build whatever's for dinner around one of them instead of planning a whole new recipe every night.
>
> Concretely: scrambled eggs + beans + whatever veg is wilting in the fridge is a 25g+ protein dinner in under 10 minutes, no plan required. Greek yogurt + a spoon of peanut butter + frozen berries is basically dessert that also hits your number. The trick isn't a better recipe, it's having 2-3 "no-recipe" combos memorized so you're never starting from zero on a tired weeknight.
>
> I ended up collecting the combos and simple recipes that worked best for me into a short cookbook, "Protein Without the Grind" — mentioning it here because most high-protein content assumes you have an hour and a meal-prep container collection, and a lot of us just don't. The anchor-ingredient approach above works whether or not you ever open the book.
