## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The one batching trick that got real dinner on the table for my family in under 20 minutes (even on exhausted weeknights)

**Telo príspevku:**

> For months my weeknight routine was either takeout or something sad and separate for every family member because nobody's "fast dinner" matched anyone else's. What actually fixed it was cooking two things in the same basket at staggered times instead of treating the air fryer as a one-dish-at-a-time appliance.
>
> The rule that changed everything: put whatever takes longest in first (chicken thighs, bone-in pieces), then add the fast-cooking side (frozen veggies, garlic bread, even frozen fries) partway through so both finish together instead of one going cold while you wait on the other. Second trick: line the basket with parchment with holes cut in it when you're doing two different foods, so flavors and drippings don't mix but cleanup is still one basket, one rinse.
>
> My standard "too tired to think" dinner now is chicken thighs in at 380°F for 10 minutes, then a layer of frozen broccoli added on top for the last 8 minutes — everything done at once, one basket, actual vegetables involved. I put a bunch of these paired timing combos together in a short cookbook called "Air Fryer Family Dinners in 20 Minutes" because I got tired of doing the math myself every night. Happy to share specific pairings if anyone's stuck on what goes with what.
