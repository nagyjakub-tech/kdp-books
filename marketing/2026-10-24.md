## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1 alebo r/loseit)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** My one Sunday prep session saved me from "I'm too full to cook, I'll just skip eating" on weeknights

**Telo príspevku:**

> One thing nobody warned me about on this medication: the appetite drop doesn't just mean smaller meals, it means almost zero motivation to cook *anything* by Tuesday night. For a while that meant skipping meals entirely, which is its own problem when you're already not getting enough protein in.
>
> What fixed it for me was moving all the "deciding" to one single hour on Sunday. I portion out 4-5 small, grab-and-go containers — things like a hard-boiled egg + a few turkey slices + cucumber, or a small container of Greek yogurt with a scoop of protein powder already mixed in — so that on a low-appetite night the only decision left is "open container, eat." No chopping, no stove, no talking myself into cooking when I have zero interest in food.
>
> I ended up writing down the exact rotation of small prep-ahead meals that worked for me as a short cookbook, "GLP-1 Meal Prep Sundays" — sharing it here because this sub gets the "I know I should eat but I don't want to deal with it" problem better than anyone, not trying to push it on anyone. The one-hour Sunday portioning trick above works with or without the book.
