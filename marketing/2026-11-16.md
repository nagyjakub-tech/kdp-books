## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The air fryer protein math nobody tells you: cook time changes a lot once you go past ~30g protein per portion

**Text:**
Switched to tracking protein per meal a while back and kept getting
inconsistent air fryer results once portions got denser/higher-protein
(thicker chicken breasts, double portions of fish, protein-packed
turkey meatballs) compared to the lighter stuff most air fryer recipes
are written for. What actually helped: (1) for anything over ~6oz of
lean protein, drop the temp about 10-15°F and add 3-5 minutes rather
than cranking heat to "cook it faster" — denser cuts dry out on the
outside before the inside catches up otherwise. (2) pat protein-heavy
cuts fully dry before seasoning, since the higher moisture content in
leaner, denser meat is the main reason it steams instead of crisping.
(3) flip at the halfway point every time for anything above ~1in thick
— it's not optional the way it is with thin cutlets.

Ended up writing down the exact times/temps that worked across chicken,
fish, turkey, and egg-based dishes aimed at 30g+ protein per portion as
*Air Fryer High Protein*, mostly to stop guessing every week. Anyone
else doing high-protein air fryer cooking find different tweaks that
made a bigger difference?
