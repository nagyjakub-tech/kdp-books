## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The vegan air fryer trick nobody tells you (it's not the oil, it's the moisture)

**Text:**
Spent way too long getting soggy, sad results trying to air-fry vegan
stuff — tofu, chickpeas, breaded cauliflower, you name it. Turns out the
oil amount almost never was the problem; the moisture still sitting in
the ingredient before it hits the basket was. Pressing tofu for real (not
the lazy 5-minute paper-towel version — more like 20-30 min under
something heavy), and patting breaded veggies fully dry before the
coating goes on, made a bigger difference than any amount of extra oil
spray. Also: cooking in a single, not-overcrowded layer matters way more
for vegan stuff than for meat, since there's no fat rendering out to help
things crisp if they're piled up.

Wrote down the moisture-prep steps and timing combos that actually work
across different plant proteins (tofu, tempeh, chickpeas, jackfruit) as
*Air Fryer Vegan*, mostly so I'd stop guessing every time. Anyone else
found a trick that fixed the "why is my vegan air fryer food always
mushy" problem?
