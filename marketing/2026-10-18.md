## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The one step that finally got my tofu actually crispy in the air fryer (no deep frying, no excess oil)

**Telo príspevku:**

> For a long time my air-fried tofu came out either soggy in the middle or rubbery on the outside, and I assumed tofu just wasn't a great air fryer ingredient. Turns out the problem was moisture, not the appliance — tofu holds a lot of water, and skipping the press step is basically why most people's first attempt disappoints them.
>
> Press extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes (stack something heavy on a paper-towel-wrapped block, or use a tofu press if you have one), cube it, toss with a tablespoon of cornstarch and a light coat of oil, then air fry at 400°F for 15-18 minutes, shaking the basket halfway. The cornstarch is what gives you the shatter-crisp exterior instead of a chewy one.
>
> Same cornstarch trick works for chickpeas (dry them well after rinsing, 390°F for 15 minutes) and for cauliflower florets if you toss them in a bit of flour or cornstarch before seasoning. None of this requires any animal products or deep frying, which is the whole premise of a plant-based air fryer book I put together called "Air Fryer Vegan" — but the cornstarch trick alone is worth trying even if you never look at the book.
