## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** The "lazy protein" trick that finally got me hitting my macros
without meal prepping every Sunday

**Text:**
I used to think hitting protein targets meant either buying a ton of
pre-made meals or doing the full Sunday meal-prep-Tupperware-army thing.
Both burned me out within a few weeks, every single time.

What actually stuck was switching to what I now call "one-pan protein
stacking" — pick one protein that reheats well (rotisserie chicken,
a tray of baked tofu, or ground turkey browned in bulk), and build
2-3 completely different meals off it during the week just by swapping
the sauce and sides. Same base ingredient, zero "meal prep Sunday" marathon,
and way less dishes than cooking from scratch every night.

The other thing that helped: stop treating protein powder as a snack and
start treating it as a backup for the one meal a day you know you'll be
rushed (usually breakfast or a late lunch). Takes the pressure off hitting
the number perfectly at every single meal.

I actually put this whole lazy-but-effective system together in a short
cookbook called *The Lazy Lifter's Protein Cookbook* after enough people
asked how I kept it up — happy to talk through the approach here either
way, works regardless of what book (if any) you use.
