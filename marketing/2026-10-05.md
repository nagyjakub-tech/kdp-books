## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** My go-to 5-minute high-protein breakfast when I'm out the door in 10 minutes

**Telo príspevku:**

> Mornings are chaos at my place, so I stopped pretending I'd ever cook a "real" breakfast on weekdays. My fallback: Greek yogurt (0% or 2%, whatever's cheaper that week) + a scoop of cheap whey or a spoon of peanut butter + frozen berries + a drizzle of honey. Stir, eat, done — about 25-30g protein and under $1.50 a serving if you buy yogurt in the big tub.
>
> The trick that actually made this stick for me was prepping the dry/frozen parts (berries pre-portioned in bags, protein scoops pre-measured in a little container) on Sunday so weekday-me just dumps and stirs. Zero dishes besides a bowl.
>
> If anyone wants more no-cook/5-minute high protein breakfast ideas like this, I ended up collecting a bunch of them into a cheap little cookbook ("Protein Breakfast in 5 Minutes") after getting tired of googling the same thing every week — happy to share more recipes in comments too if people want specifics.
