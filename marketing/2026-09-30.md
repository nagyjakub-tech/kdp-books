> in most people's fridge (eggs, canned beans, cottage cheese, chicken

> containers stacked to the ceiling" aesthetic — just repeatable, boring
> dinners that happen to hit 30g+ protein.
>
> I ended up collecting the ones that worked best into a small book
> called *Protein Without the Grind*, in case it saves someone else the
> months of trial and error — but honestly the bigger takeaway is: if
> meal prep burns you out, the fix isn't more discipline, it's fewer
> steps per meal.
