## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** My $5/meal protein trick that actually keeps me full (no fancy
supplements)

**Text:**
Been trying to hit 120g+ protein a day without blowing my grocery budget,
and the thing that finally worked was batch-cooking one "protein base"
every Sunday: a tray of baked chicken thighs (cheaper than breast, just as
much protein per dollar), a pot of lentils, and a dozen eggs. I portion
them into containers and mix-and-match through the week — chicken + lentils
+ rice one day, eggs + lentils + toast the next. Each meal lands around
35-40g protein for roughly $4-5 total, and because it's not the same exact
dish every day, I don't get bored of it like I did with just "chicken and
rice" every single meal.

The other thing that helped was tracking cost-per-gram-of-protein instead
of just price — eggs and canned tuna punch way above their weight there,
even on a tight budget.

If anyone wants the actual weekly breakdown I used to plan this out, I put
it together in a short cookbook called *High Protein on $5 a Meal* — happy
to share more of the recipes here too if people want specifics.
