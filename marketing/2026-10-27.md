**Navrhovaný titulok:** Eating well on GLP-1 without the grocery bill tripling (budget tips that actually worked)

**Telo príspevku:**

> One thing that surprised me on this medication: everyone online pushes protein powders, pre-made shakes, and "clean" convenience food that quietly adds $100+ a month to groceries — right when you're already eating less and maybe working less too.
>
> What actually worked was leaning on the cheapest protein sources instead of the trendiest ones: eggs, canned tuna, dried beans, cottage cheese, frozen chicken thighs on sale. >
> I ended up writing down the combinations and rough costs that got me through this cheaply as a short cookbook, "GLP-1 on a Budget" — mentioning it here because most GLP-1 meal content assumes you can afford specialty ingredients, and that's just not where a lot of us are. The cheap-protein-first approach above works with or without the book.
