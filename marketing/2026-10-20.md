## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** What actually helped me hit protein goals when I could only eat a few bites (small-plate ratio, not a diet plan)

**Telo príspevku:**

> One of the hardest parts of GLP-1 meds for me wasn't willpower — it was that my stomach would only take a few bites before I was done, and most "healthy eating" advice assumes you can eat a normal-sized plate. What helped was flipping the question from "what should I eat" to "what's the smallest amount of food that still gets me 20-25g of protein."
>
> My go-to: 3oz of a dense protein (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, a boiled egg + a bit of chicken, or a protein shake if solids aren't happening that day) eaten *first*, before anything else, while I still have appetite left. Everything else — veggies, carbs — is optional bonus, not the main event anymore. Pairing a bland protein with something with a little acid or crunch (a squeeze of lemon, a few pickles) also helped with the nausea-adjacent "don't want to eat anything" days.
>
> I ended up writing down the exact small-portion, high-protein combos that worked for me on the worst nausea days into a short cookbook called "Eating on GLP-1 Without Hating It" — mentioning it here because this sub is exactly who it's for, not to push it on anyone. The core idea above (protein first, in tiny amounts, before appetite runs out) is free and works with or without the book.
