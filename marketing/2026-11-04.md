## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** Hit 100g+ protein without meat — here's the combo that actually
works (not just "eat more tofu")

**Text:**
Going vegetarian (or just cutting back on meat) made hitting my protein
target way harder than I expected, until I stopped relying on one "hero"
ingredient and started stacking smaller protein sources on purpose.

The combo that actually moved the needle for me: Greek yogurt or cottage
cheese as a base for breakfast (15-20g right there), a legume + grain pairing
at lunch (lentils + rice, or chickpeas + quinoa — the two complement each
other so you're not just getting incomplete protein), and eggs or tofu as
the "flexible" dinner protein depending on what's cheap that week. None of
these is a protein bomb on its own, but stacked across the day they add up
fast, and it's way cheaper than buying a ton of specialty meat substitutes.

I ended up writing all the combos and meal ideas down in a small cookbook
called *High Protein Zero Meat* because I kept explaining this same stacking
logic to people — happy to share specific recipes here too if anyone wants
details on a particular meal.
