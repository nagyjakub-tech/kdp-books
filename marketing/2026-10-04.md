## Reddit hodnotový post — r/EatCheapAndHealthy

**Titulok:** Vegetarian and tired of hitting 40g protein by 8pm? Here's what finally worked

**Text:**
> Spent way too long thinking "high protein" meant chicken breast, and
> as a vegetarian that left me stuck with bland tofu stir-fries on
> repeat. What actually moved the needle: stacking *two* protein sources
> per meal instead of relying on one — e.g. lentils + a fried egg on
> top, or Greek yogurt + a scoop of peanut butter in oats, or tofu
> scrambled with eggs instead of either alone. Cottage cheese stirred
> into pasta sauce is an underrated one too — nobody notices it's there
> and it adds 10-15g for basically free. Also worth tracking for just a
> week: most of us underestimate how little protein breakfast has and
> overestimate dinner.
>
> I collected the combos that actually kept me full in a short cookbook
> called *High Protein Zero Meat*, mostly so I'd stop reinventing the
> same five meals — but the stacking trick works regardless of what
> book you use: pick two protein sources per meal, not one, and
> breakfast is usually your weakest link.
