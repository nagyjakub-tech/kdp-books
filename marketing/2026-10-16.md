## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The air fryer trick that actually got my protein per dollar up (not just "add chicken breast")

**Telo príspevku:**

> Everyone says "just eat more chicken breast" for protein on a budget, but boneless skinless chicken breast isn't even the cheapest cut, and it dries out fast in an air fryer if you're not careful. What moved the needle for me was switching to chicken thighs (bone-in when on sale) and eggs as the two anchors, then using the air fryer for texture, not just "cooking it through."
>
> Thighs at 380°F for about 20-22 minutes come out with crispy skin without needing extra oil, and the fat that renders off actually bastes the meat so it doesn't dry out like breast does. Egg "muffins" (whisked eggs + whatever veg is about to go bad, poured into a silicone muffin tray that fits the basket) at 320°F for 12 minutes give you a grab-and-go protein source that's basically free per gram compared to protein bars.
>
> Doing the math on cost per gram of protein changed which cuts I buy entirely — it's not always the "diet" cut that wins. I ended up collecting all the protein-per-dollar numbers and air fryer times for the cuts I actually buy into a book called "Air Fryer High Protein" if anyone wants the exact breakdown, but even just switching your anchor protein is worth trying on its own.
