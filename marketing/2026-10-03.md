## Reddit hodnotový post — r/EatCheapAndHealthy

**Titulok:** Stopped failing at meal prep once I accepted I'm lazy and planned for it

**Text:**
> For years my meal prep plan was "cook five days of food on Sunday,"
> and it always collapsed by Wednesday because I hate spending two
> hours in the kitchen on a day off. What actually stuck: picking 3-4
> high-protein meals that use almost the same base ingredients (eggs,
> canned beans, pre-cooked chicken, frozen veg) and just rotating the
> sauce/seasoning, so grocery shopping and cooking both take way less
> mental effort. Also cheating with a rice cooker and a sheet pan
> instead of juggling multiple pans — fewer dishes means I actually do
> it again the next week instead of giving up.
>
> I ended up writing down the exact rotation that worked for me in a
> short cookbook called *The Lazy Lifter's Protein Cookbook*, mostly so
> I'd stop re-figuring it out every few months — but the core idea
> works regardless of what book you use: pick a small base ingredient
> list, keep the technique boring and repeatable, and optimize for
> "will I actually do this again" over "is this an interesting recipe."
