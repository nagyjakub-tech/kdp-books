## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** The freezer-meal trick that finally got me to stop skipping
protein on busy nights

**Text:**
For months my "too tired to cook" fallback was cereal or toast, which meant
zero protein on exactly the nights I needed it most after a long day. What
fixed it wasn't willpower, it was prepping the freezer, not the fridge.

Once a month I make a few big batches of things that actually freeze and
reheat well with the protein intact — turkey chili, shredded chicken +
rice portions, bean-and-cheese burrito bags, lentil soup with extra
protein powder stirred in before freezing. Portion into flat freezer bags
or containers, lay flat to freeze fast, and label with the date. On a bad
night it's microwave 4-5 minutes, done, 30+g protein, less than $2 a
portion.

I put together a batch-cooking + freezer-safe recipe collection called
*High Protein Freezer Meals* since I kept refining the "does this actually
reheat well" list — happy to share which proteins hold up best in the
freezer if anyone's curious (hint: ground meat and beans great, anything
cream-sauce based, not so much).
