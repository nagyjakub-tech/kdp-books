## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The 3 things I wish someone told me before I bought an air fryer (zero clue beginner here)

**Text:**
Got an air fryer last month with absolutely no idea what I was doing, burned
a batch of frozen fries black on the first try. Things that actually helped
once I figured them out: (1) preheat for 2-3 minutes just like an oven —
skipping this is why food comes out soggy instead of crispy. (2) don't
overcrowd the basket — if pieces are touching, shake/flip halfway or do two
smaller batches, overlapping food steams instead of crisping. (3) start
checking doneness 3-4 minutes before the recipe says, every air fryer runs a
little different in wattage and basket size, so "12 minutes" on a box is a
starting point, not gospel.

Wrote all the beginner-proof timing/temp stuff down properly after I got
tired of guessing, ended up in a little book called *Air Fryer Zero Clue*
aimed at people starting from literally nothing — happy to answer any
"is this normal" air fryer questions here if anyone else is just getting
started.
