## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** What actually stayed down for me on the bad nausea days (and what made it worse)

**Telo príspevku:**

> The first few weeks on this medication, "what should I eat" turned into "what won't come back up." I learned fast that anything rich, greasy, or strongly scented was a gamble, even foods I used to love. The ones that consistently worked were room-temperature, bland, and small: dry toast with a thin layer of peanut butter, plain scrambled eggs, cold cottage cheese, saltines with a little cheese. Anything hot and steamy out of the microwave was more likely to set off the nausea than anything else — the smell alone could do it.
>
> The hardest part was still trying to hit any kind of protein number on days like that, since most "easy" sick-day foods (broth, toast, crackers) have basically none. Cold, bland, protein-forward swaps for the usual sick-day foods made the biggest difference — things like cottage cheese instead of applesauce, a plain hard-boiled egg instead of dry cereal.
>
> I wrote down the combinations that got me through those weeks as a short cookbook, "GLP-1 Nausea-Proof Recipes" — mentioning it here because this sub knows exactly what I mean about the smell thing, not to push it on anyone. The cold-and-bland swap above costs nothing and helps with or without the book.
