## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** Turning 40 made me realize "just eat less" was the wrong
advice — here's what actually worked

**Text:**
For years my go-to for weight stuff was just cutting calories, and
somewhere in my late 30s I noticed I was losing weight but looking
softer, not leaner — turns out that's classic sign of losing muscle
along with fat, which gets way easier to do after 40 if protein isn't
high enough.

What fixed it for me, on a normal grocery budget, was two small changes:
leading every meal with the protein source instead of treating it as a
side (so dinner starts with "what protein am I eating" not "what sounds
good"), and hitting 25-30g protein at breakfast specifically, since that's
the meal I used to skip or just have coffee. Eggs, Greek yogurt, and
leftover chicken from the night before covers that without any powders
or supplements.

It sounds small but tracking it for a month showed I was eating almost
double the protein I used to at the same calorie level, and energy in
the afternoon noticeably improved too.

I ended up writing down the exact meal structure I use in a short
cookbook called *High Protein After 40* if anyone wants the specifics —
happy to answer questions about the approach here either way.
