## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** How I hit 150g of protein a day without my grocery bill exploding

**Telo príspevku:**

> When I started actually tracking macros, the thing that almost broke my budget wasn't food in general — it was protein specifically. Chicken breast and protein powder add up fast. What actually worked was leaning on the "ugly" cheap cuts and bulk staples instead of the Instagram-friendly ones: chicken thighs instead of breast (cheaper per pound, more forgiving to cook), a 10lb bag of dried lentils split into weekly portions, canned tuna/mackerel on sale, a big tub of plain cottage cheese instead of flavored protein snacks, and eggs by the flat (18-30ct) instead of small cartons.
>
> Cooking in bulk once a week also helped way more than I expected — one tray of baked thighs, one pot of lentils, one batch of hard-boiled eggs, and then I just mix-and-match through the week instead of cooking from scratch every day.
>
> I ended up tracking all my go-to budget protein combos (cost per gram of protein, not just per meal) and turned it into a little cookbook called "The Broke Bodybuilder's Cookbook" after a few lifting buddies kept asking what I actually eat — happy to share specific recipes/numbers in the comments if anyone's curious.
