## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** Finally stopped air frying 4 portions when I only need 1 — here's what actually works

**Text:**
Living alone, I kept scaling down "family size" air fryer recipes and
either undercooking everything or ending up with sad leftovers nobody
wants. What fixed it: (1) cook by thickness, not weight — a single chicken
thigh needs the same time as four as long as they're not stacked, so I
stopped multiplying cook times for no reason. (2) frozen single portions
(one salmon fillet, one personal pizza) go straight in cold, no thaw, just
add 2-3 minutes. (3) basket liners cut to size mean zero cleanup for a
one-person dinner, which is honestly what keeps me actually cooking
instead of ordering out.

Ended up writing these down properly in a little book called *Air Fryer
for One* since "family of 4" recipes kept not working for a household of
1 — happy to share more single-portion timing tricks here if anyone's
stuck on a specific protein or veggie.
