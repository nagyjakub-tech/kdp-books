## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** How I keep freezer meals from turning into sad rubbery protein bricks

**Telo príspevku:**

> Freezer meal-prep always used to burn me — chicken that turned to rubber, sauces that separated, rice that went mushy on reheat. What finally worked: I only freeze things *before* the final cook step when possible (raw marinated chicken/ground turkey in a flat freezer bag lies flatter, freezes faster, thaws faster), and I freeze sauces/grains separately from the protein so nothing overcooks together. For ground meat dishes (chili, turkey meatballs, bolognese) I actually prefer freezing fully cooked since it reheats fine.
>
> Portioning into single-serving flat bags instead of one big tub also made a huge difference — thaws overnight in the fridge instead of needing the microwave defrost setting, which is usually what wrecks the texture.
>
> I ended up writing down my whole freezer rotation (what freezes raw vs. cooked, reheat times, 25g+ protein per serving) into a little cookbook called "High Protein Freezer Meals" after my group chat kept asking for my method — happy to share specific recipes in the comments if anyone wants one.
