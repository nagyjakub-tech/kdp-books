## Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Titulok:** What actually helped me eat enough protein when food sounds
awful (not meal replacement shakes)

**Text:**
The hardest part of the first few months for me wasn't cravings, it was
the opposite — almost nothing sounded appealing and I'd forget to eat
until I felt shaky. What helped was switching to very small, protein-
forward plates I could eat in 5-10 minutes without feeling like I was
"doing a meal": a soft-scrambled egg with a spoon of cottage cheese,
or a few bites of shredded chicken in broth. Bigger plates were
actually working against me because they felt overwhelming before I'd
even started.

I ended up collecting the small-portion, easy-on-the-stomach recipes
that got me through that stretch into something I called *Eating on
GLP-1 Without Hating It* — happy to share a few of the exact combos here
if it helps anyone else who's in the "nothing sounds good" phase right
now.
