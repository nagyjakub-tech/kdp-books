## Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Titulok:** What actually helped my nausea — it's not ginger tea, it's
how you build the plate

**Text:**
Everyone told me ginger tea and small sips of water, and sure, that
helps a little, but the bigger shift for me was realizing *fat* and
*spice* were the real nausea triggers on GLP-1, not food volume. Once I
started building meals around plain-grilled protein, starchy-but-bland
carbs (rice, toast, plain potato), and basically zero added oil, the
nausea dropped off a lot faster than when I was "eating light" but still
adding olive oil and garlic to everything.

I started keeping a running list of the combos that never triggered
nausea for me and ended up turning it into something called *GLP-1
Nausea-Proof Recipes* — happy to share a few of the actual recipes here
if anyone's still in the "can barely finish a meal" phase.
