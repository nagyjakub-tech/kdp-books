## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The "broke budget" air fryer trick that actually saves money (it's not about cheap cuts, it's about batch-cooking one basket right)

**Text:**
Been cooking on a tight grocery budget for a while and the thing that
actually moved the needle wasn't switching to cheaper protein (chicken
thighs over breasts, etc. — everyone already knows that one). It was
treating the air fryer basket as a single batch unit instead of
single-serving: filling it to capacity with whatever's on sale (frozen
veggies, a tray of thighs, halved potatoes) in one 20-minute run instead
of three small runs across the week. Cuts both the electricity and the
"oh I'll just order out" temptation that hits when cooking feels like
too much effort for one portion. Also learned the hard way that
parchment liners aren't optional with sauce-heavy cheap cuts — saved me
probably $20 in replaced basket coatings over a year.

Wrote down the exact combos and timings that work for batch-cooking
budget cuts (what reheats well vs. what gets soggy, which frozen
vegetables actually crisp vs. turn to mush) as *Air Fryer on a Broke
Budget*, mostly so I'd stop relearning it every grocery cycle. Curious
if others have found batch-size tricks that made a bigger dent than
ingredient swaps.
