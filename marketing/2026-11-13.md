## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** The "20-minute family dinner" air fryer rotation that actually survived 4 picky eaters

**Text:**
Spent way too long treating the air fryer like a snack machine before I
realized it could actually carry whole family dinners on a weeknight. A
few things that made the biggest difference once I started planning
around it instead of just reheating leftovers in it: (1) batch-prep the
protein and veg separately earlier in the day — the air fryer is fast, but
chopping four kids' worth of chicken at 5:45pm is not — so staggering prep
removes the actual bottleneck. (2) two-basket or two-stage cooking (protein
first, keep warm, then the sides) beats trying to cram everything in at
once and getting soggy results. (3) picking 5-6 "boring but reliable"
meals you rotate weekly kills the nightly "what do we even make" paralysis
way more than Pinterest-hunting for novelty every night.

Eventually wrote all of this down as an actual rotation plan with timing
for each dish, called it *Air Fryer Family Dinners in 20 Minutes* — happy
to swap notes here on what's worked for other people's weeknight chaos too.
