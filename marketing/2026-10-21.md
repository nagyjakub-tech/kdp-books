## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The plate-size mistake I kept making on GLP-1 (and the simple fix that stopped the food waste)

**Telo príspevku:**

> For the first couple months on my GLP-1 med, I kept plating "normal" portions out of habit, eating three bites, and then staring at a plate of food I knew I wasn't going to finish. It felt wasteful and honestly a little discouraging, like I was doing something wrong.
>
> What actually fixed it was plating on a smaller dish from the start — a side plate or even a saucer instead of a dinner plate — and building the portion backward from a protein target instead of a food-group checklist. I aim for roughly a palm-sized protein (egg, a few ounces of chicken, cottage cheese) as the anchor, then add only as much else as still looks "full" on that smaller plate. Visually it reads as a complete meal instead of a sad half-eaten one, which made a bigger difference to how I felt about eating than I expected.
>
> I ended up collecting the small-plate versions of meals that worked for me into a short cookbook called "GLP-1 Small Plates" — mentioning it here because this sub is exactly who it's for, not to push it on anyone. The plate-size trick above is free and works with or without the book.
