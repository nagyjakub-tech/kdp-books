## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** The lunchbox formula that finally got me to stop buying $14
sad desk salads

**Text:**
For months my "meal prep" was just cooking chicken and rice and getting
so bored of it by Wednesday that I'd cave and buy lunch. What fixed it
wasn't a new recipe, it was a formula: 1 protein base (rotate between
shredded chicken, chickpeas, egg, or tuna), 1 cold carb that doesn't go
soggy (farro, orzo, or roasted sweet potato — never rice, it dries out),
1 crunchy veg, and 1 sauce packed separately so nothing gets soggy by
lunchtime. Mix and match those four slots and you never eat the "same"
lunch twice even though you're prepping one big batch on Sunday.

I ended up writing all these grab-and-go combos down into a little book
called *High Protein Lunchbox* because I kept texting the formula to
coworkers who asked what I was eating — happy to share more of the
protein-base/carb/crunch/sauce combos here if people want specifics.
