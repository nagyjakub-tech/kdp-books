## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** The "no prep, no plan" air fryer dinner that saved my weeknights (one pan, ~20 min, whatever's in the fridge)

**Telo príspevku:**

> I used to get home from work, open the fridge, and just order delivery because cooking felt like one more task I didn't have energy for. What actually fixed it wasn't a recipe — it was a ratio I stopped having to think about.
>
> Cut whatever protein you have (chicken thighs, sausage, tofu, even frozen shrimp) into even pieces, toss with whatever vegetables are closest to going bad (bell peppers, zucchini, broccoli, onion all work), coat lightly in oil and one seasoning blend you already like, and air fry at 400°F for 15-20 minutes, shaking the basket once at the halfway mark. That's it — no marinating, no separate sides to time, no recipe card.
>
> The trick that actually makes it taste intentional instead of "whatever was in the fridge": cut everything to roughly the same size so it finishes together, and don't overcrowd the basket or you'll get steamed instead of crisp (do two batches if needed). This exact no-decisions approach is the whole premise behind a book I put together called "Air Fryer After Work," but the ratio above works with zero book required — just wanted to share what finally got me cooking again on weeknights.
