## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** PSA: your frozen food section is secretly an air fryer meal kit (stop defrosting)

**Text:**
Realized a while back that I was wasting way more time than necessary
"meal planning" when half my freezer was already basically dinner —
frozen veggies, nuggets, fries, dumplings, you name it. A few things that
made a real difference once I stopped winging it: (1) never defrost first —
straight from frozen to the basket gives a crisp exterior, thawing first
just makes everything mushy and steamy. (2) frozen food needs a slightly
longer cook than the bag says if you're not preheating, but a shorter one
if you are — check at the halfway mark instead of trusting the box timing
blindly. (3) shake or flip every batch once, frozen pieces stick together
way more than fresh ones and clump into soggy spots if you don't.

Ended up writing down all the specific times/temps for the stuff I actually
buy (veggies, proteins, snacks, the works) because I got tired of
Googling mid-meal, turned into a little book called *Air Fryer Frozen
Aisle Hacks* — happy to just answer freezer-to-air-fryer questions here too
if anyone's stuck on a specific item.
