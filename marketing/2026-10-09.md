## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** I stopped buying $3 protein bars and started making my own — way cheaper and they don't taste like chalk

**Telo príspevku:**

> I used to buy a box of protein bars every week just to get through the 3pm slump at work, and it was quietly costing me $25-30/month for bars I didn't even enjoy eating. Switched to making a few snacks in batches on Sunday instead and it's both cheaper and actually tastes like food.
>
> My current rotation: baked cottage cheese flatbread cut into strips (surprisingly good dipped in hot sauce), no-bake peanut butter and oat protein balls rolled in cocoa powder, hard-boiled eggs seasoned ahead with everything bagel seasoning so they're not boring, and a simple Greek yogurt + whey + frozen berry "bark" I freeze in a sheet pan and break into pieces. All of them hit 12-18g protein per serving and keep for the week in the fridge.
>
> The trick that made it stick was batching one snack every Sunday instead of trying to meal-prep everything at once — 20 minutes, one snack type, done. I ended up writing all my go-to combos down after a few coworkers kept asking what I was eating, in a little book called "Protein Snacks That Aren't Cardboard" — happy to drop the cottage cheese flatbread recipe in the comments if anyone wants it, it's the one people ask about most.
