## Reddit hodnotový post (r/airfryer)

**Titulok:** Stopped turning on the oven for dessert once I figured out the air fryer timing for this

**Text:**
For the longest time I treated the air fryer as a "dinner only" appliance
and still preheated the whole oven just for one dessert, which felt
ridiculous in hindsight. A few things that changed once I actually tried
dessert in it: (1) lower temp than you'd think — most baked desserts
(cookies, small cakes, stuffed fruit) do better at 320-330°F in an air
fryer than the oven temp on the box, because the air circulation cooks
faster at the surface. (2) parchment liners or a light oil spray matter
way more here than for savory food — sugar sticks hard to a bare basket.
(3) check at the halfway mark, not the end — air fryer desserts go from
"not done" to "burnt edges" faster than you expect the first few times.

Ended up writing down the temps/times I landed on for the ones that kept
working (apple hand pies, molten mug-cake style brownies, cinnamon
bananas) as *Air Fryer Desserts* — happy to swap notes if anyone else has
a dessert that keeps burning on them in theirs.
