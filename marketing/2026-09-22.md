## Reddit hodnotový post — r/airfryer

**Titulok:** The single-serving air fryer trick that finally stopped me wasting food

**Text:**
> I used to just eyeball everything and either burn my food or end up with
> a cold, soggy middle when cooking for just myself. What actually fixed it
> for me: stop using the same time/temp as recipes written for a full
> batch. For a single chicken breast or a cup of frozen veg, drop the temp
> about 15°F below what the recipe says and check at the 60% mark instead
> of waiting for the full time — small portions cook faster and carry over
> heat more aggressively once you pull the basket. Shaking the basket
> halfway through also matters way more at this scale, since there's less
> food to buffer uneven airflow.
>
> Also — pat everything fully dry before it goes in. Single portions have a
> much higher surface-area-to-moisture ratio, so any extra water is the
> difference between crispy and steamed.
>
> I ended up writing down all the ratios I use daily because I kept
> forgetting them — turned it into a little book called *Air Fryer for One*
> if anyone else cooking solo wants the cheat sheet, but honestly the temp
> drop + halfway shake tip above is the free version that solves 90% of it.
