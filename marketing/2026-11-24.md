## Reddit hodnotový post (r/GLP1)

**Titulok:** My Sunday meal prep rule that fixed the "forgot to eat all
day" problem

**Text:**
The thing nobody warns you about on GLP-1 isn't overeating, it's the
opposite — you get busy, the appetite cue never fires, and suddenly it's
8pm and you've had coffee and nothing else. What fixed it for me was
doing one small-batch prep session every Sunday: 4-5 single-portion
containers, each one built around one protein + one easy-to-tolerate
carb/veg, nothing that needs reheating-and-stirring effort on a bad day.
Pre-portioning is the actual trick — if the container is already the
"right" small amount, I don't have to negotiate with myself about
portions when I'm not hungry anyway.

I ended up writing down my whole rotation of go-to prep meals as *GLP-1
Meal Prep Sundays* — happy to drop a couple of the actual combos here if
anyone wants a starting point for their own Sunday prep.
