## Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Titulok:** Finally found protein snacks that don't taste like sawdust (and cost less than bars)

**Text:**
I used to spend way too much on protein bars that tasted like sweetened
chalk, so I started making my own snacks instead. Three that actually
stuck: (1) Greek yogurt bark — spread thick yogurt on a tray, top with
crushed nuts and a drizzle of honey, freeze, snap into pieces, ~10g
protein per piece and tastes like dessert. (2) Cottage cheese "dip" with
everything bagel seasoning and cucumber spears — sounds weird, tastes
incredible, 14g protein per half cup. (3) Hard-boiled eggs tossed in hot
sauce and a pinch of salt, kept in the fridge for grab-and-go.

I collected a bunch of these into a little book called *Protein Snacks
That Aren't Cardboard* because I got sick of explaining the yogurt bark
thing to people at work every week — happy to share more combos here if
anyone wants specifics for a particular macro target.
