## 2. Reddit hodnotový post (r/EatCheapAndHealthy)

**Navrhovaný titulok:** How I got my protein number up without my grocery bill following it

**Telo príspevku:**

> Everyone acts like hitting 100g+ protein a day means buying pre-cooked chicken strips and protein powder at $15 a bag. The stuff that actually moved my protein-per-dollar number was boring: dried beans and lentils (pennies per serving, 15-18g protein a cup cooked), eggs (still one of the cheapest protein sources per gram anywhere), canned tuna/sardines on sale, and chicken thighs instead of breast — same protein, way cheaper per pound.
>
> The actual shift that helped was tracking protein-per-dollar for a week instead of protein-per-meal — it made it obvious that a $0.30 bowl of beans and rice with an egg on top was beating a $9 "protein bowl" from the fridge aisle by a mile, both in protein and in not going broke.
>
> I put the combos and cheap recipes that worked for me into a short book, "High Protein on $5 a Meal" — sharing here because most high-protein advice assumes a bigger grocery budget than a lot of us actually have. The staples above work with or without the book.
